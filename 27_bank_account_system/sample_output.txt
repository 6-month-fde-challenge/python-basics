==============================================================
  1. Opening accounts
==============================================================
    BankAccount.get_total_accounts()  -> 0

      BankAccount('Aarav Sharma', 'AC1001', 25000.0)
      BankAccount('Diya Menon', 'AC1002', 8400.5)
      BankAccount('Kabir Rao', 'AC1003', 0.0)
      BankAccount('Meera Nair', 'AC1004', 1000.0)

    BankAccount.get_total_accounts()  -> 4

    Kabir's account opened at 0.00 - the default argument.
    Meera's opened at 1,000.00 without main.py naming the figure:
    open_salary_account() is a class method that calls cls(...).

==============================================================
  2. deposit(), withdraw(), check_balance()
==============================================================
    before
      Aarav Sharma    AC1001       25,000.00
      Diya Menon      AC1002        8,400.50
      Kabir Rao       AC1003            0.00
      Meera Nair      AC1004        1,000.00

    aarav.deposit(5000)      ->    30,000.00
    aarav.withdraw(12000)    ->    18,000.00
    diya.deposit(1599.50)    ->    10,000.00
    kabir.deposit(3000)      ->     3,000.00
    meera.withdraw(250)      ->       750.00

    after
      Aarav Sharma    AC1001       18,000.00
      Diya Menon      AC1002       10,000.00
      Kabir Rao       AC1003        3,000.00
      Meera Nair      AC1004          750.00

    Every call changed exactly one object. deposit() and withdraw()
    return the new balance, so a caller never has to guess.

==============================================================
  3. The validation - you cannot take out what is not there
==============================================================
    Kabir's balance: 3,000.00

    more than the balance           InsufficientFundsError
                                      cannot withdraw 9,999.00 from a balance of 3,000.00 (short by 6,999.00)
    exactly one rupee over          InsufficientFundsError
                                      cannot withdraw 3,001.00 from a balance of 3,000.00 (short by 1.00)
    a negative amount               InvalidAmountError
                                      invalid amount -500.0: must not be negative
    zero                            InvalidAmountError
                                      invalid amount 0.0: must be greater than zero
    a string                        InvalidAmountError
                                      invalid amount '2000': must be a number
    True                            InvalidAmountError
                                      invalid amount True: must be a number
    above the per-transaction limit InvalidAmountError
                                      invalid amount 60000.0: above the per-transaction limit of 50,000.00

    Kabir's balance is still 3,000.00 - a refused
    withdrawal changes nothing. The check happens before the
    subtraction, not after it.

    And the boundary that is allowed:
      kabir.withdraw(3000)   -> 0.00

==============================================================
  4. transfer_to() - two objects in one call
==============================================================
    before   Aarav    18,000.00   Diya    10,000.00
    aarav.transfer_to(diya, 4000)
    after    Aarav    14,000.00   Diya    14,000.00

    A transfer that cannot be afforded moves nothing:
      InsufficientFundsError: cannot withdraw 40,000.00 from a balance of 14,000.00 (short by 26,000.00)
      balances before (14000.0, 14000.0)
      balances after  (14000.0, 14000.0)

    withdraw() runs first and raises, so deposit() is never reached.
    Ordering the two calls the other way round would credit Diya
    with money Aarav never had.

==============================================================
  6. The class variable and the class method
==============================================================
    BankAccount.bank_name  -> Nova Bank
    Every account reads the same one:
      Aarav Sharma    Nova Bank
      Diya Menon      Nova Bank
      Kabir Rao       Nova Bank

    BankAccount.rename_bank('Nova Federal Bank')
    BankAccount.bank_name  -> Nova Federal Bank

      Aarav Sharma    Nova Federal Bank
      Diya Menon      Nova Federal Bank
      Kabir Rao       Nova Federal Bank

    Four objects changed and not one of them was touched, because
    the name was never copied into them. rename_bank() assigns to
    cls.bank_name - the single shared copy.

    The trap, for contrast:
      meera.bank_name = 'Rogue Bank'
      meera.bank_name        -> Rogue Bank
      BankAccount.bank_name  -> Nova Federal Bank
      aarav.bank_name        -> Nova Federal Bank
    Assigning through the object made an instance variable that
    hides the class variable - for that one account only.
      del meera.bank_name    -> Nova Federal Bank  (the shared one is back)

    BankAccount.total_accounts        -> 4
    BankAccount.get_total_accounts()  -> 4

==============================================================
  4 accounts, one bank name, one counter.
  Not one rupee left an account that did not have it.
